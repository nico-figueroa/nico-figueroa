## About

I am a systems-driven engineering and technology professional with experience across financial services, medical devices, manufacturing, and software development. My career at Western Union, Hospira, Volcano Corporation, and Philips has centered on designing, stabilizing, and improving complex operational and product-lifecycle systems — integrating safety, security, quality, regulatory, and risk-management frameworks for both hardware and software products.

I hold a Master of Science in Information Management (MSIM) from the University of Washington, graduating with a 4.0 GPA, and a Bachelor’s in Electrical Engineering. My academic foundation in mathematics, statistics, systems analysis, and information management complements professional certifications in Software Validation, Risk Management Integration, and Full-Stack Engineering. I am currently progressing through Codecademy’s AI Engineer Career Path, expanding my capabilities in neural networks, machine learning, and intelligent automation.

My technical portfolio — available on GitHub — reflects hands-on experience with Python, JavaScript, Node.js, Express, React, SQL, PostgreSQL, OAuth, REST APIs, and AI applications. I’ve prototyped full-stack systems, secure web architectures, RAG pipelines, and automation solutions that bridge software, data, and operational workflows.

I thrive where disciplines intersect. My strength is systemic thinking and cross-functional integration: understanding how complex systems behave, identifying patterns across domains, and translating ambiguity into structured, reliable, scalable solutions. As a continuous learner, I stay current with emerging technologies, frameworks, and industry trends — ensuring my knowledge evolves alongside the systems I build and support.

With a career that spans industries and technical domains, I bring versatility, adaptability, and a holistic engineering mindset — ready to contribute wherever systems, data, and intelligent automation meet.
